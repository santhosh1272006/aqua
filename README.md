# AquaZen — 20-Liter Water Can Delivery Website

A responsive, multi-page static website for a 20-liter drinking water can delivery business.

## Pages
1. Home — `index.html`
2. About Us — `about.html`
3. How It Works — `how-it-works.html`
4. Pricing — `pricing.html`
5. Delivery Areas — `delivery-areas.html`
6. Contact Us — `contact.html`
7. Login — `login.html`
8. Register — `register.html`
9. Terms & Conditions — `terms.html`
10. Privacy Policy — `privacy.html`

## Run locally
No build tool is required.

1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open the project folder.
3. Double-click `index.html`.
4. The site opens in Google Chrome.

For a local development server, from the project folder run:
- Python: `python -m http.server 8000`
- Then visit `http://localhost:8000`

## Important production notes
This is a production-ready **front-end/static implementation**. Login, registration, order submission and contact forms currently show a demo success message. Before taking real customer data, connect them to a secure backend/API, database, authentication provider and server-side validation.

Replace the sample:
- Pricing
- Delivery areas
- Phone number
- Email address
- Legal wording
- Business/service information

with your actual AquaZen business details.
